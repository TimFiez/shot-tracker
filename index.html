About
Shot Tracker analyzes target groups using the PRB centroid method.
R95 = Mean Radius × 2.1, per PrecisionRifleBlog.

Save backup creates a file you can store anywhere — tap "Save to Files" on iPhone and choose iCloud Drive to sync across all your Apple devices.

Works offline. Add to home screen for an app-like experience.
